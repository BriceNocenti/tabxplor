Phase 9b-2 -- fmt-materialization cost decomposition (throwaway measurement spike)
==================================================================================

GOAL: decide the Phase 9b-3 go/no-go (defer the single new_fmt materialization to the end of the
build) by decomposing tab()'s per-table build cost -- how much is the IRREDUCIBLE build-once
(new_fmt of the final columns) vs the RECOVERABLE fmt-record reconstruction paid through
join / slice / rbind / set_* between the leaf and the final table.

WHY THIS IS A MEASUREMENT, NOT the roadmap's "plain-field CI/chi2 writers" implementation:
on the common color="diff" path -- the primary exploratory workflow AND the decisions.md §29
profiling fixture -- the 9b-2 writers recover ~0: tab_ci never runs (ci defaults to "no";
color="diff", unlike "diff_ci"/"after_ci", does NOT force a CI -- R/tab-resolve.R:96), and
tab_chi2 on color_ctr="no" writes NO fmt records (only the `test` tibble, already plain via
agg_chi2). tab_num already computes CI+pvalue on plain vectors before its single new_fmt. So the
writers bite ONLY on CI (ci="diff"/color_signif/stars) and contrib tables; the DOMINANT per-table
cost -- the leaf new_fmt build + its vctrs reconstruction through the pipeline -- is recoverable
only by 9b-3. Measuring 9b-2's writers alone would read ~0 on the common fixture and mis-gate.

Harness: dev/benchmarks/phase9b2_fmt_cost_decomp.R (NO R/*.R change). forcats::gss_cat (21 483
rows), 5 row_vars (marital,race,relig,rincome,partyid); devtools::load_all live source; R 4.5.1;
bench::mark medians. Four representative shapes; output_list=TRUE isolates the no-merge per-row_var
build (tab_compact skipped).

  fixture | col_vars              | args
  common  | race,marital,partyid  | pct="row", color="diff", chi2=TRUE      (= §29 fixture)
  ci      | race,marital,partyid  | + ci="diff"        (exercises tab_ci 3x set_* / col)
  contrib | race,marital,partyid  | color="contrib"    (exercises tab_chi2 set_var/set_ctr)
  numeric | age,tvhours           | color="diff"       (means; tab_num path)


PART 1 -- per-table build floor + merge share (median wall seconds; 5 iters)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
  fixture  merge_s  list_s  merge_delta_s  per_table_s   (per_table_s = list_s / 5 row_vars)
  common    1.62     1.44       0.18          0.288
  ci        2.18     1.84       0.33          0.369
  contrib   2.71     2.36       0.35          0.473
  numeric   0.185    0.143      0.042         0.0285

  merge_s = default tab() (runs tab_compact); list_s = tab(output_list=TRUE) = the per-row_var
  build sum (no merge). merge_delta is noisier run-to-run (common 0.18-0.28 s across runs); the
  0.16 s vec_rbind remainder inside tab_compact after 9b-1 is separately recoverable by carrying
  the merge on plain columns (9b-3). CI adds +28% / contrib +64% to the per-table build vs common
  -- the extra is exactly the tab_ci / tab_chi2 record writes 9b-2 targets.

  Reading: on the common FACTOR build, vec_restore* (29.7%) is the single largest cost -- the
  tabxplor_fmt record being REBUILT after each vctrs op (slice/c/join). Add vec_case_when (18.1%,

  The "fmt-primitive self (LB)" row is a hard LOWER BOUND (self-time never double-counts; more
  hides in .Call).


PART 3 -- micro cross-check: materialize-once floor vs reconstruct-through-ops (representative table)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
One real per-table result (8 rows x 21 fmt columns), 30 iters:

  materialize-once floor  (new_fmt x 21 cols, from plain field-frames) :   1.4 ms
  reconstruct 6 rounds -- fmt records (vec_c + vec_slice)              : 233   ms
  reconstruct 6 rounds -- plain frames + ONE final materialize          :   4.3 ms
  overhead removed by deferral (fmt - plain)                          : 229   ms   (54.5x)
  common per_table build (Part 1)                                     : 288   ms

  DECISIVE: building the records ONCE is ~1.4 ms = 0.5% of the 288 ms per-table build -- the
  build-once is NOT the cost. Pushing those records through the SAME ops as plain field-frames is
  54.5x slower. So essentially ALL the fmt cost is REDUNDANT reconstruction (the leaf materializes,
  then every join/slice/rbind rebuilds the record), which deferral eliminates by keeping plain
  columns until one final materialization. Corroborates Part 2's vec_restore 29.7%.


VERDICT -- GO for 9b-3 (defer materialization); FOLD the 9b-2 writers into it (no separate rung)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Recoverable share on the COMMON path: ~30% (vec_restore alone) to ~48% (+ vec_case_when) of the
  per-table build -- comfortably above the 25% bar. Part 3 confirms the mechanism (record ops
  54.5x plain; build-once floor ~0.5%). This CLEARS the go/no-go.
- The share is LARGER for CI (+28%) and contrib (+64%) tables, and -- per decisions.md §27 --
  larger still at big table-of-tables / warm-jmvtab scale (where the aggregate is cached and only
  the O(cells) fmt build/reconstruct remains). gss_cat's small 8x21 tables are the conservative
  case; the win grows with cells.
- NUMERIC-only tables gain little (cost = the data.table scan; tab_num already materializes once).
  9b-3's win is FACTOR-path-specific -- which is the common exploratory workflow.
- 9b-2 as a SEPARATE committable rung (local plain tab_ci/tab_chi2 writers) is NOT worth it: the
  writer round-trips are a subset of the 9b-3 carrier win (9b-3 flows plain fields THROUGH ci/chi2
  too), and a local unwrap->rewrap in tab_ci would be partly reworked by 9b-3. Fold the plain
  writers into 9b-3; keep 9b-2 as this measurement.

CROSS-VALIDATION: Part 2 (vec_restore ~30% of the build) and Part 3 (record reconstruction 54.5x
plain, floor negligible) agree -- the pipeline pays ~30% in record reconstruction that shrinks
~55x under deferral. The go/no-go does not rest on the heuristic self-bucketing (discarded).
